## How to Evaluate a Licensed Provider

Choosing the right **cleaning and pest control company UAE** businesses can depend on requires verifying regulatory compliance and technical transparency before signing an agreement:

1. **Verify Municipal Licensing**: Confirm active accreditation from authorities such as Dubai Municipality, Sharjah Municipality, or Tadweer (Abu Dhabi Waste Management Center).
2. **Request Safety Documentation**: Ask for Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) to ensure all pesticides and solvents are odorless, biodegradable, and safe for children and pets.
3. **Inspect Warranty & SLA Terms**: Reliable contractors provide written post-treatment follow-ups and clear Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
